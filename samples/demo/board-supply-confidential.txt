STRICTLY CONFIDENTIAL

Board-level supply agreement

1. Parties. This agreement is between Najd Industrial Supplies Co. ("Supplier") and Ramla Retail Co. ("Customer").

2. Supply. The Supplier will deliver the goods listed in Schedule 1 to the Customer's warehouses in Riyadh and Jeddah.

3. Payment. The Customer pays each undisputed invoice within 45 days of receipt.

4. Liability. Each party's total liability under this agreement is limited to the fees paid in the 12 months before the claim.

5. Governing law. This agreement is governed by the laws of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, and the courts of Riyadh have jurisdiction.
