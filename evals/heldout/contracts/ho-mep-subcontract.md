# MEP Subcontract — Jeddah Distribution Centre, Phase 2

This Subcontract is made on 3 March 2027 between Al-Bina Al-Raeda Contracting Co., a limited liability company registered in Riyadh under Commercial Registration No. 1010487221 (the "Contractor"), and Gulf Spectrum Electromechanical Est., registered in Jeddah under Commercial Registration No. 4030392117 (the "Subcontractor"). The Contractor is the main contractor for the Jeddah Distribution Centre, Phase 2 (the "Project") and engages the Subcontractor to design, supply, install, test and commission the mechanical, electrical and plumbing installations described in Schedule 1 (the "Works").

The Subcontract Price is SAR 9,600,000 (nine million six hundred thousand Saudi riyals), exclusive of VAT, and is fixed and not subject to escalation. The Works shall reach practical completion within fourteen (14) months of the date of the Contractor's notice to proceed, followed by a defects liability period of twelve (12) months.

The Contractor shall pay an advance of ten per cent (10%) of the Subcontract Price against an unconditional advance payment guarantee issued by a bank licensed by the Saudi Central Bank, to be recovered pro rata from interim payments. The Subcontractor shall submit monthly applications for payment, and the Contractor shall pay each certified amount within forty-five (45) days of receiving the corresponding valid tax invoice, less a retention of five per cent (5%), one half of which is released at practical completion and the other half at the end of the defects liability period.

The Contractor may, at any time and for its convenience, terminate this Subcontract in whole or in part by giving the Subcontractor thirty (30) days' written notice. The Subcontractor shall then be paid for work properly executed and materials delivered to site up to the effective date of termination, and shall have no claim for loss of profit on the unexecuted balance or for any other termination charge.

Each party shall indemnify the other against claims brought by third parties for death, personal injury or damage to property to the extent caused by the negligence or breach of statutory duty of the indemnifying party, its personnel or its sub-suppliers.

Sums for which the Contractor may become answerable to the Subcontractor under or in connection with this Subcontract, including for prolongation, standby of labour and plant, or loss of anticipated profit, are recoverable in full and shall not be reduced by reference to any financial threshold. The Subcontractor's total exposure to the Contractor under this Subcontract, howsoever arising and whether in contract, tort or otherwise, is confined to ten per cent (10%) of the Subcontract Price.

Shop drawings, as-built drawings, test certificates and operation and maintenance manuals prepared specifically for the Project become the property of the Contractor upon payment of the relevant interim certificate. The Subcontractor retains ownership of its standard details, design software and calculation templates, and grants the Contractor and the Project owner a perpetual, irrevocable, royalty-free licence to use them for the construction, operation, maintenance and extension of the Project.

Each party shall keep confidential all drawings, prices and commercial information received from the other in connection with the Project, and this obligation shall continue for four (4) years after practical completion.

Neither party may assign this Subcontract or any right under it without the prior written consent of the other. The Subcontractor shall not sub-let more than thirty per cent (30%) of the value of the Works without the Contractor's prior written approval of each sub-subcontractor.

This Subcontract is governed by the laws of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. Any dispute not settled amicably within thirty (30) days shall be finally resolved by arbitration administered by the Saudi Center for Commercial Arbitration before three arbitrators, seated in Riyadh and conducted in Arabic.
