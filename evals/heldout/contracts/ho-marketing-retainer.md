# Integrated Marketing Agency Retainer Agreement

This Agreement is made between Al-Noor Logistics Co., Commercial Registration No. 1010622348, Riyadh (the "Client"), and Najd Creative House Co., Commercial Registration No. 1010711460, Riyadh (the "Agency"). The Agency shall provide brand strategy, campaign creative, media planning and social media management for the Client's "Al-Noor Express" last-mile delivery brand, in accordance with the annual scope of work in Appendix 1.

This Agreement runs for twelve (12) months from 1 January 2027 and expires on 31 December 2027 without further action by either party. Any renewal requires a new written agreement signed by both parties.

The Client may terminate this Agreement at any time on forty-five (45) days' written notice without penalty. On such termination the Client shall pay only the retainer accrued pro rata up to the effective date of termination, and no other sum.

The retainer is SAR 85,000 per month, exclusive of VAT, invoiced monthly in arrears. The Client shall pay each valid invoice within thirty (30) days of receiving it. Media spend is billed at cost, without mark-up, and only against a media plan the Client has approved in writing in advance. No advance payment is required.

All creative work, copy, artwork, video, campaign concepts and other materials produced by the Agency for the Client under this Agreement (the "Deliverables") belong to the Client upon creation, and the Agency hereby assigns to the Client all rights in them. The Agency retains ownership of its pre-existing templates, tools and know-how, and grants the Client a perpetual, royalty-free, non-exclusive licence to use any of them incorporated in the Deliverables. The Client's data and customer lists remain the Client's property at all times.

Where the Agency processes customer contact lists or other personal data on the Client's behalf, it shall do so only on the Client's documented instructions and in accordance with the Personal Data Protection Law, keep the data on servers located in the Kingdom, transfer none of it outside the Kingdom without the Client's prior written consent and appropriate safeguards, notify the Client of any personal data breach within seventy-two (72) hours, and delete the data within thirty (30) days after this Agreement ends.

Each party's obligations of confidentiality continue for five (5) years after this Agreement ends, and for trade secrets for as long as they remain secret.

Each party's aggregate liability arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall not exceed the fees paid or payable under this Agreement in the twelve (12) months preceding the event giving rise to the claim. This cap does not apply to breach of confidentiality, fraud, wilful misconduct, or death or personal injury.

During the term, the Agency shall not provide campaign services to any courier or last-mile delivery company operating in the Kingdom without the Client's prior written consent. Nothing in this Agreement restricts the Client from engaging other agencies for any purpose.

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, and the courts of Riyadh shall have jurisdiction over any dispute arising from it.
