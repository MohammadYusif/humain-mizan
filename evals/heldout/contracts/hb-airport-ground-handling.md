# Ground Handling Services Agreement

This Agreement is made between Najd Ramp Services Company, a Saudi limited liability company ("the Supplier"), and Riyadh Regional Air Company ("the Customer"). The Supplier shall provide ramp handling, baggage handling, passenger boarding and aircraft turnaround services for the Customer's flights at King Khalid International Airport, Riyadh, in accordance with the service levels in Schedule 1 and the requirements of the General Authority of Civil Aviation.

This Agreement starts on 1 January 2027 and continues for twelve months. It ends automatically at the close of that period. Any extension requires a new written agreement signed by authorised signatories of both parties; there is no automatic renewal.

The Supplier shall invoice the Customer monthly in arrears for services actually performed. The Customer shall pay each undisputed invoice within forty-five days after receiving it. No advance payment, deposit or mobilisation fee is payable. If an undisputed invoice is still unpaid after its due date, the Supplier may charge a single administrative fee equal to 0.5% of the overdue amount; no further charge of any kind accrues on late amounts.

Each party shall act in the interest of passenger, crew and aircraft safety at all times. The Supplier shall promptly disclose to the Customer any conflict of interest arising from services it provides to other carriers at the same airport. Nothing in this Agreement prevents the Customer from appointing other ground handlers at this or any other airport.

The Customer may terminate this Agreement for convenience at any time by giving the Supplier thirty days' written notice, without paying any termination fee, penalty or compensation beyond the charges for services performed up to the termination date. Either party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other party commits a material breach and fails to remedy it within twenty-one days after being notified of it.

The Supplier shall process the personal data of passengers and crew that it receives under this Agreement only on the Customer's documented instructions and in accordance with the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law and its implementing regulations. That data shall be stored on servers located in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia and shall not be transferred outside the Kingdom without the Customer's prior written consent and documented safeguards. The Supplier shall notify the Customer of any personal data breach within forty-eight hours of becoming aware of it.

All flight records, load sheets, baggage reconciliation data, reports and other materials produced for the Customer under this Agreement belong to the Customer. The Supplier keeps ownership of its pre-existing handling software and procedures and grants the Customer a non-exclusive, royalty-free licence to use any of them that are embedded in those materials.

Each party shall indemnify the other against claims brought by third parties to the extent they arise from the indemnifying party's negligence, its breach of the data protection obligations in this Agreement, or its infringement of a third party's intellectual property rights.

The total liability of each party to the other, including the liability of the Customer to the Supplier, arising out of or in connection with this Agreement, whether in contract, tort or otherwise, shall not exceed the total fees paid and payable under this Agreement in the twelve months preceding the event giving rise to the claim. This limit does not apply to liability for fraud, wilful misconduct, breach of confidentiality, or death or personal injury caused by negligence.

Each party shall keep the other party's confidential information secret and use it only for the purposes of this Agreement. This obligation continues for four years after this Agreement ends, and for trade secrets for as long as they remain trade secrets.

Neither party may assign or transfer any of its rights or obligations under this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other party.

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. Any dispute arising out of or in connection with it shall be finally resolved by arbitration administered by the Saudi Center for Commercial Arbitration, seated in Riyadh, under its rules in force at the time.
