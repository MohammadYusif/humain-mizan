# Solar Plant Operation and Maintenance Agreement

Sahra Photovoltaic Services LLC ("the Contractor") shall operate, clean, inspect, repair and maintain the 40 MWp ground-mounted photovoltaic plant at Sakaka, Al-Jouf, owned by Al-Jouf Clean Power Company ("the Owner"), including the inverters, trackers, transformers and monitoring systems, in accordance with the scope and maintenance schedule in Annex A.

The initial period of this Agreement is twenty-four months from the Commencement Date. Thereafter the arrangement carries forward in successive blocks of twelve months, each block beginning of its own accord, save where the Owner signals in writing, no later than seventy-five days before the initial period or the current block runs out, that it does not wish to continue.

The Contractor guarantees a monthly plant availability of at least 99 per cent, measured as set out in Annex B. For each full percentage point by which availability falls below that level in a month, the Owner shall receive a service credit of 2 per cent of the monthly fee for that month, deducted from the next invoice.

The Contractor shall invoice the monthly fee in arrears at the end of each calendar month. The Owner shall pay each undisputed invoice within thirty days after receiving it. No mobilisation advance or other prepayment is payable.

Any sum the Owner has not settled by its due date shall carry an additional charge, building up at two per cent for each calendar month or part of a month that passes until the sum is cleared in full.

Each party shall keep confidential all technical, commercial and operational information received from the other under this Agreement and use it only to perform this Agreement. This duty lasts for three years after this Agreement ends, and for trade secrets for as long as they remain secret.

All operating data, SCADA records, inspection reports, thermographic and drone imagery and other deliverables produced under this Agreement belong to the Owner. The Contractor keeps its own pre-existing tools, software and methods and grants the Owner a perpetual, irrevocable, royalty-free licence to use any of them embedded in the deliverables.

The Owner may terminate this Agreement for convenience at any time on sixty days' written notice, without penalty, paying only the fees for services performed up to the termination date. Either party may terminate this Agreement if the other commits a material breach and does not remedy it within thirty days after written notice.

Neither the Owner nor the Contractor shall be liable to the other, in contract, tort or otherwise, for more than an amount equal to the fees paid and payable under this Agreement in the twelve months before the event giving rise to the claim. This limit does not apply to fraud, wilful misconduct, breach of confidentiality, or death or personal injury.

The Contractor may not assign or subcontract this Agreement, in whole or in part, without the Owner's prior written consent. The Owner may assign this Agreement to a successor owner of the plant on written notice to the Contractor.

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. Any dispute arising from it shall be referred to arbitration under the rules of the Saudi Center for Commercial Arbitration, with the seat of arbitration in Riyadh.
