# Data Centre Colocation Services Agreement

Jeddah Gateway Data Centres Company ("the Provider") shall provide Red Sea Logistics Holding Company ("the Customer") with four secured cabinets in Data Hall 2 of its Jeddah facility, together with metered power, cooling, cross-connects, physical security and remote hands support, as described in the Service Order.

This Agreement runs for an initial term of twelve months from the service start date and then renews for further periods of twelve months each, unless either party gives the other written notice of non-renewal at least thirty days before the end of the current term.

Monthly recurring charges are invoiced on the first day of each month, and the one-time installation charge is invoiced on the service start date. The Customer shall pay each undisputed invoice within thirty days after receiving it.

The Provider shall maintain power availability of 99.99 per cent and keep temperature and humidity in the data hall within the ranges set out in the Service Level Schedule. If it does not, the Customer is entitled to the service credits in that schedule, without prejudice to its other rights.

The Customer may terminate this Agreement for convenience on sixty days' written notice, paying only the charges accrued up to the termination date and no early termination fee.

The Provider shall not access the Customer's equipment or the data stored on it except at the Customer's written request. Any personal data the Provider processes, including visitor access logs and CCTV footage, shall be processed in accordance with the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law, kept within the Kingdom, and any breach shall be notified to the Customer within seventy-two hours.

The Provider's aggregate liability under or in connection with this Agreement is limited to the charges paid by the Customer in the twelve months before the event giving rise to the claim. No equivalent ceiling applies to the Customer: the Customer shall make good, in full and without regard to amount, every loss, cost, expense and damage the Provider suffers in connection with the Customer's equipment, personnel or use of the facility, howsoever arising.

Each party shall keep the other's confidential information secret during this Agreement and for five years after it ends, and trade secrets for as long as they remain secret.

The Customer keeps full ownership of its equipment and of all data held on it. Nothing in this Agreement gives the Provider any right, title, licence or lien over the Customer's equipment or data.

Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other's prior written consent, except that either party may assign it to an affiliate on written notice.

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, and the competent courts of Jeddah have jurisdiction over any dispute arising from it.
