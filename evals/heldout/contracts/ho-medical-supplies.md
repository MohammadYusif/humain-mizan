# Supply Agreement for Orthopaedic Implants and Surgical Consumables

This Agreement is made between Shifa Al-Jazeera Hospitals Co., Commercial Registration No. 1010554902, which operates three hospitals in Riyadh and Al-Kharj (the "Hospital"), and MedAxis Trading Co. Ltd., Commercial Registration No. 1010339871, holder of a medical device establishment licence issued by the Saudi Food and Drug Authority (the "Supplier"). The Supplier shall supply on consignment the orthopaedic implants, instrument sets and surgical consumables listed in Annex 1, at the prices in Annex 2, from its licensed warehouse in Riyadh.

This Agreement takes effect on 1 April 2027 and remains in force for thirty-six (36) months. It shall not renew automatically; any extension requires a written amendment signed by both parties.

The Supplier may terminate this Agreement for any reason on ninety (90) days' prior written notice. The Hospital may terminate this Agreement only if the Supplier commits a material breach and fails to remedy it within thirty (30) days of written notice specifying the breach, or if the Supplier's SFDA licence is suspended or revoked.

The Supplier shall invoice monthly for items implanted or consumed during the preceding month, as recorded in the signed consignment report. The Hospital shall pay each undisputed invoice within ninety (90) days of its receipt by the Hospital's accounts payable department. No advance payment shall be required for consignment stock.

To meet SFDA traceability requirements, the Hospital will record, for each implant used, the lot number together with the patient's name, medical record number and date of surgery on the Supplier's web-based consignment portal (the "Traceability Data"). The Supplier may store, replicate and process the Traceability Data on infrastructure operated by itself or by any of its group companies or service providers, wherever situated, and may relocate it between such locations from time to time as it sees fit.

The Supplier shall defend and indemnify the Hospital against any claim by a third party that the supply or use of the products in accordance with their instructions for use infringes a patent, trade mark or other intellectual property right.

Neither party's aggregate liability under this Agreement shall exceed the total amounts paid and payable by the Hospital in the twelve (12) months immediately preceding the claim, except that this limitation shall not apply to breach of confidentiality, fraud, wilful misconduct, or death or personal injury.

Each party shall keep confidential the other party's pricing, clinical and commercial information, and this obligation survives for five (5) years after termination or expiry of this Agreement.

Neither party may assign or transfer this Agreement without the other party's prior written consent, save that the Supplier may assign it to an affiliate holding the required SFDA licences upon written notice to the Hospital.

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. Any dispute shall be finally settled by arbitration under the rules of the Saudi Center for Commercial Arbitration, with the seat of arbitration in Riyadh.
