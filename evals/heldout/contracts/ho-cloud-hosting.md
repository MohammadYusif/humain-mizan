# Managed Cloud Hosting Services Agreement

This Agreement is made between Al-Noor Logistics Co., Commercial Registration No. 1010622348, Riyadh (the "Customer"), and Sahab Data Centres Co., Commercial Registration No. 1010598876, a registered cloud service provider (the "Provider"). The Provider shall host and manage the Customer's warehouse management system and EDI gateway on dedicated virtual infrastructure in the Provider's Riyadh region, as described in the Service Description in Annex A.

This Agreement has an initial term of twenty-four (24) months from the go-live date. It then renews for successive periods of twelve (12) months unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least thirty (30) days before the end of the current period.

The Customer may terminate this Agreement for convenience on sixty (60) days' written notice without penalty. On any termination the Provider shall provide reasonable exit assistance and return all Customer Data in a standard, machine-readable format within thirty (30) days.

The monthly service fee is SAR 142,500, exclusive of VAT, invoiced monthly in arrears. The Customer shall pay each undisputed invoice within thirty (30) days of receipt. Service credits for availability below the levels in Annex B shall be deducted from the next invoice.

Customer Data, including the personal data of the Customer's employees, drivers and consignees, shall be processed in accordance with the Personal Data Protection Law and stored in the Provider's primary data centre in Riyadh, with backup copies replicated to its disaster-recovery site in Dammam. No Customer Data shall be transferred outside the Kingdom except to the secondary disaster-recovery region in Bahrain listed in Annex C, and then only with the Customer's prior written consent and under the documented safeguards set out in that Annex. The Provider shall notify the Customer of any personal data breach within twenty-four (24) hours of discovering it.

Each party's total liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the fees paid or payable in the twelve (12) months preceding the event giving rise to the claim. This limit does not apply to breach of confidentiality, fraud, wilful misconduct, or death or personal injury.

The Customer retains all rights in the Customer Data and in any configurations, scripts and documentation developed specifically for the Customer under this Agreement. The Provider retains its hosting platform, automation tooling and pre-existing know-how.

Each party shall keep the other's confidential information secret during the term and for five (5) years after this Agreement ends.

Neither party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other party.

Notices under this Agreement shall be in writing, in Arabic or English, and delivered by hand, by courier or by email to the addresses in Annex D, and take effect on receipt.

This Agreement, together with its Annexes, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties on its subject matter, and may be amended only in writing signed by the authorised signatories of both parties.
