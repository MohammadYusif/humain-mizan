# Fleet Maintenance and Repair Services Agreement

This Agreement is entered into between Al-Noor Logistics Co., Commercial Registration No. 1010622348, Riyadh (the "Customer"), and Al-Mutawa Motors Workshop Co., Commercial Registration No. 2050118734, Dammam (the "Provider"). The Provider shall carry out preventive maintenance, breakdown repair and 24/7 roadside recovery for the Customer's fleet of 240 heavy trucks and 65 light vans operating from its depots in Riyadh, Dammam and Jeddah, in accordance with the service levels set out in Schedule A.

This Agreement commences on 1 February 2027 and continues for an initial period of thirty-six (36) months. On expiry of the initial period, and of each subsequent period, it shall roll over for a further twenty-four (24) months unless either party serves notice of non-continuation no later than one hundred and twenty (120) days before the date on which the then-current period would otherwise end.

Either party may bring this Agreement to an end at any time, without cause, on ninety (90) days' written notice to the other. No early-exit fee, minimum-volume shortfall or other compensation shall be payable by reason of such termination.

Labour is charged at the hourly rates in Schedule B and parts at the Provider's published list price less twelve per cent (12%). The Provider shall issue one consolidated invoice by the fifth day of each month for work completed in the preceding month. The Customer shall pay undisputed amounts within forty-five (45) days of receipt of the invoice, and shall notify any disputed item within fifteen (15) days, after which the parties shall resolve it in good faith.

Any undisputed amount that remains outstanding after its due date shall carry a finance charge of eighteen per cent (18%) per annum, calculated daily on the outstanding balance from the due date until the date of actual payment.

Each party's total liability under this Agreement in any contract year is limited to the charges paid or payable by the Customer in the twelve (12) months preceding the event giving rise to the claim. This limit does not apply to liability for death or personal injury, fraud, wilful misconduct or breach of confidentiality.

The Provider shall indemnify the Customer against claims by third parties, including other road users, arising from defective workmanship or from parts fitted by the Provider. The Customer shall indemnify the Provider against third-party claims arising from the Customer's continued operation of a vehicle that the Provider has notified in writing as unroadworthy.

The Provider may access the Customer's telematics platform solely to diagnose faults. Any personal data of drivers obtained in doing so shall be processed in accordance with the Personal Data Protection Law, kept on servers located in the Kingdom, and not transferred abroad. The Provider shall notify the Customer of any personal data breach within forty-eight (48) hours of becoming aware of it.

Each party shall treat the other's commercial and operational information as confidential during the term and for three (3) years after its termination or expiry.

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. The competent commercial courts in Riyadh shall have exclusive jurisdiction over any dispute arising out of or in connection with it.
