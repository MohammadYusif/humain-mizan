# Freight Forwarding and Customs Clearance Services Agreement

This Agreement is made between Al-Noor Logistics Co., Commercial Registration No. 1010622348, Riyadh (the "Customer"), and Red Sea Cargo Forwarders Co., Commercial Registration No. 4030288813, Jeddah, a licensed customs broker (the "Forwarder"). The Forwarder shall arrange sea and air freight, customs clearance at Jeddah Islamic Port, King Abdulaziz Port in Dammam and King Khalid International Airport, and onward delivery to the Customer's warehouses, as instructed in individual booking requests.

The initial term of this Agreement is twelve (12) months from 1 March 2027. Thereafter it renews automatically for successive periods of twelve (12) months unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least sixty (60) days before the end of the then-current period.

Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience on ninety (90) days' written notice, without any termination fee or penalty.

The Forwarder shall invoice each shipment upon delivery. The Customer shall pay undisputed invoices within seventy-five (75) days after receipt. Customs duties, VAT and port charges paid by the Forwarder on the Customer's behalf shall be reimbursed at cost, against the official ZATCA and port receipts, within the same period.

If an undisputed invoice remains unpaid fifteen (15) days after its due date, the Forwarder may levy a one-time administrative fee of SAR 500 or one half of one per cent (0.5%) of the overdue amount, whichever is lower. No other late-payment charge, interest or compensation for delay shall be payable.

Each party's aggregate liability under this Agreement is limited to the total fees paid or payable in the twelve (12) months before the claim arose. There shall be no limit on either party's liability for fraud, wilful misconduct, breach of its confidentiality obligations, or death or personal injury caused by its negligence.

The Forwarder shall indemnify the Customer against any third-party claim alleging that the Forwarder's shipment-tracking software or documentation infringes the intellectual property rights of that third party. The Customer gives no indemnity under this Agreement.

Each party shall keep confidential the other's rates, shipment volumes and customer information, and this obligation survives for seven (7) years after this Agreement ends.

Either party may assign this Agreement to an affiliate, or to a successor to all or substantially all of its business, on written notice to the other party. Any other assignment requires the other party's prior written consent.

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. Disputes shall be finally resolved by arbitration under the rules of the Saudi Center for Commercial Arbitration, seated in Riyadh.
