# Master SaaS Subscription Agreement between Nimbus Cloud Ltd ("Supplier") and Al-Noor Logistics Co. ("Customer")

1. Services. Supplier shall provide the Nimbus Fleet Analytics platform (the "Service") to Customer in accordance with the Order Form and the service levels in Schedule 1.

2. Fees and Payment. Customer shall pay all undisputed invoices within thirty (30) days of receipt. Fees are fixed for the initial term.

3. Late Payment. Overdue amounts shall bear a late charge of 1.5% per month, compounded monthly, until paid in full.

4. Term and Renewal. This Agreement commences on the Effective Date for an initial term of three (3) years and shall automatically renew for successive three (3) year terms unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least one hundred and twenty (120) days before the end of the then-current term.

5. Termination. Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon sixty (60) days' written notice. Either party may terminate immediately for the other's uncured material breach.

6. Intellectual Property. Customer retains all rights in Customer Data. Supplier retains all rights in the Service. Reports generated for Customer through the Service are owned by Customer.

7. Confidentiality. Each party shall protect the other party's Confidential Information with reasonable care for five (5) years after termination of this Agreement.

8. Data Protection. Supplier may process and store Customer Data, including personal data of Customer's employees and drivers, in any data centre worldwide selected by Supplier, without prior notice to Customer.

9. Limitation of Liability. Customer's aggregate liability arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall be unlimited, including for indirect and consequential losses. Supplier's aggregate liability shall not exceed the fees paid by Customer in the three (3) months preceding the claim.

10. Governing Law. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, USA, and the courts of Delaware shall have exclusive jurisdiction over any dispute.
