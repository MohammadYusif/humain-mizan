# Consulting Services Agreement between Al-Noor Logistics Co. ("Client") and Meridian Advisory FZ-LLC ("Consultant")

1. Services. Consultant shall provide the supply-chain optimisation services described in Statement of Work 1 (the "Services") for a fixed term of twelve (12) months from the Effective Date. This Agreement does not renew automatically.

2. Fees and Payment. Client shall pay each undisputed invoice within ninety (90) days of receipt.

3. Termination. Consultant may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon thirty (30) days' written notice. Client may terminate this Agreement only for Consultant's uncured material breach.

4. Intellectual Property. All deliverables created specifically for Client under this Agreement are owned by Client upon payment. Consultant retains its pre-existing materials and grants Client a perpetual, royalty-free licence to use them within the deliverables.

5. Confidentiality. Each party shall keep the other party's Confidential Information confidential for three (3) years after termination of this Agreement.

6. Data Protection. Consultant shall process personal data only on Client's documented instructions, in compliance with the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law, shall host such data in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia or transfer it only with Client's prior written consent, and shall notify Client of any personal data breach within seventy-two (72) hours.

7. Limitation of Liability. Each party's aggregate liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the fees paid or payable in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim, except that liability for breach of confidentiality, fraud, wilful misconduct, death or personal injury is not limited.

8. Assignment. Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other party's prior written consent.

9. Governing Law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. Any dispute shall be finally settled by arbitration under the rules of the Saudi Center for Commercial Arbitration seated in Riyadh.
