# Managed Analytics Services Agreement between Pacific Rim Data Systems Pte. Ltd. ("Supplier") and Al-Noor Logistics Co. ("Customer")

1. Services. Supplier, a company incorporated in Singapore, shall provide managed demand-forecasting and route-analytics services for Customer's distribution network, as described in Statement of Work 1.

2. Term. This Agreement has a fixed term of thirty-six (36) months from the Effective Date and does not renew automatically.

3. Fees and Payment. Customer shall pay each undisputed monthly invoice within seventy-five (75) days of receipt.

4. Late Payment. If Customer fails to pay an undisputed invoice when due, Supplier may charge a fixed administrative fee of SAR 500, once per overdue invoice. No interest accrues on overdue amounts.

5. Intellectual Property. Customer owns all Customer Data and all forecasts, models and reports produced specifically for Customer. Supplier retains its pre-existing platform and grants Customer a perpetual, royalty-free licence to use it to the extent embedded in the deliverables.

6. Indemnity. Each party shall indemnify the other against third-party claims arising from its infringement of intellectual property rights, its data breach or its negligence.

7. Termination. Supplier may terminate this Agreement for convenience on thirty (30) days' written notice. Customer may terminate this Agreement only for Supplier's uncured material breach. 8. Limitation of Liability. Each party's aggregate liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the fees paid or payable in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim. Liability for breach of confidentiality, fraud, wilful misconduct, death or personal injury is not limited.

9. Data Protection. Supplier shall process personal data in accordance with the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law, host it in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, and notify Customer of any personal data breach within seventy-two (72) hours.

10. Confidentiality. Each party shall keep the other party's Confidential Information confidential for three (3) years after termination of this Agreement.

11. Governing Law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. Any dispute shall be finally settled by arbitration under the rules of the Saudi Center for Commercial Arbitration seated in Riyadh.
