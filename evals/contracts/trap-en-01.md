# Equipment Supply and Maintenance Agreement between Hamburg Hafen Technik GmbH ("Supplier") and Al-Noor Logistics Co. ("Customer")

1. Scope. Supplier, a company incorporated in Hamburg, Germany, shall supply and install twelve (12) automated container-handling cranes at Customer's Dammam terminal and maintain them in accordance with Schedule 1.

2. Term and Renewal. This Agreement has an initial term of twelve (12) months and renews automatically for successive twelve (12) month periods unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least thirty (30) days before the end of the then-current term.

3. Termination for Convenience. Customer may terminate this Agreement for convenience at any time on sixty (60) days' written notice, without penalty or termination fee.

4. Fees and Payment. Customer shall pay each undisputed invoice within one hundred and twenty (120) days of receipt. No advance payment is required, and no interest or late charge accrues on any amount paid late.

5. Intellectual Property. Customer owns all Customer Data and all site-specific configurations and documentation produced for Customer. Supplier retains its pre-existing software and know-how and grants Customer a perpetual, irrevocable, royalty-free licence to use them with the cranes.

6. Indemnity. Supplier shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Customer against any third-party claim that the cranes or their embedded software infringe any intellectual property right. Customer gives no indemnity under this Agreement.

7. Limitation of Liability. Each party's aggregate liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the total fees paid or payable in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim. Liability for breach of confidentiality, fraud, wilful misconduct, or death or personal injury shall be unlimited for both parties.

8. Confidentiality. Each party shall keep the other party's Confidential Information confidential for five (5) years after termination, and shall protect trade secrets for as long as they remain trade secrets.

9. Data Protection. Supplier shall process any personal data in compliance with the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law, host it only in data centres located in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, and notify Customer of any personal data breach within seventy-two (72) hours.

10. Assignment. Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other party's prior written consent.

11. Governing Law and Disputes. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. Any dispute shall be finally resolved by arbitration administered by the Saudi Center for Commercial Arbitration, seated in Riyadh.
