# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement between Al-Noor Logistics Co. and Falcon Freight Systems Ltd

1. Purpose. The parties wish to exchange confidential information to evaluate a possible commercial relationship (the "Purpose").

2. Confidential Information. "Confidential Information" means non-public information disclosed by one party to the other in connection with the Purpose, in any form, that is marked confidential or would reasonably be understood to be confidential.

3. Obligations. The receiving party shall use Confidential Information only for the Purpose, protect it with at least the care it uses for its own confidential information, and disclose it only to employees and advisers who need to know and are bound by equivalent duties.

4. Term. This Agreement applies to disclosures made during twenty-four (24) months from the Effective Date. The obligations in clause 3 continue for four (4) years after the end of that period.

5. Liability. Each party's aggregate liability under this Agreement is limited to direct damages and shall not exceed five hundred thousand Saudi riyals (SAR 500,000). Liability for breach of confidentiality obligations, fraud or wilful misconduct is not limited.

6. Assignment. Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other party's prior written consent.

7. Governing Law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, and the competent courts in Riyadh have jurisdiction over any dispute.
