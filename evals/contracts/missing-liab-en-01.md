# Consulting Services Agreement between Al-Noor Logistics Co. ("Client") and Orion Analytics LLC ("Consultant")

1. Services. Consultant shall provide the data-analytics services described in Statement of Work 1 for a fixed term of nine (9) months from the Effective Date. This Agreement does not renew automatically.

2. Fees and Payment. Client shall pay each undisputed invoice within forty-five (45) days of receipt.

3. Termination. Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon sixty (60) days' written notice.

4. Intellectual Property. All deliverables created specifically for Client under this Agreement are owned by Client. Consultant retains its pre-existing materials and grants Client a perpetual, royalty-free licence to use them within the deliverables.

5. Confidentiality. Each party shall keep the other party's Confidential Information confidential for four (4) years after termination of this Agreement.

6. Data Protection. Consultant shall process personal data only on Client's documented instructions, in compliance with the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law, shall host such data in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, and shall notify Client of any personal data breach within seventy-two (72) hours.

7. Assignment. Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other party's prior written consent.

8. Governing Law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. Any dispute shall be finally settled by arbitration under the rules of the Saudi Center for Commercial Arbitration seated in Riyadh.
